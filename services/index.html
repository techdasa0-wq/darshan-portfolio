Executive Coaching

I help senior leaders build the self-awareness, presence, and judgment they need to lead well under pressure — for leaders and executives inside established organizations.

Executive coaching here covers two related but distinct engagements: leadership coaching — self-awareness, presence, and judgment for the role — and personal relationship coaching, the same discipline applied to marriage and family. Most clients start with one; some bring both into the same conversation. Each is booked separately.

Leadership Coaching

What this covers

01

Self-Mastery & Emotional Intelligence

Understanding who you are before the pressure of the role asks you to perform.

02

Leadership Communication & Presence

The clarity and presence needed to hold a room and lead a team.

03

Decision-Making & Composure

Judgment that holds up under real pressure, not just in calm conditions.

04

Professional Relationships

Stakeholder, peer, and team dynamics — the relationships that make or break execution.

Format

1:1 and confidential — typically sponsored by the individual or their organization.
