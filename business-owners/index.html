SMB Owner Coaching

I help small and medium-business owners build the systems, accountability, and leadership discipline needed to scale — for owner-operators whose business still runs through them personally, where growth is capped by their own bandwidth, not the market.

What This Covers

Three things that let a business grow past its owner

01

Systems & Delegation

SOPs, decision rights, and a clear answer to who owns what — so the business doesn't run through one person's memory.

02

Accountability Rhythm

A review cadence, real KPIs, and follow-through — so plans turn into completed work without the owner chasing every step.

03

Leadership Discipline

The owner's own operating habits, and a leadership layer built underneath them — so the business can run without the owner in every room.

Format

Retainer, Not a Workshop

This is operating-system work, not a one-off session — engagements run on a recurring monthly or quarterly cadence.
