About Darshan

Training built on lived experience, not borrowed frameworks

Over nine years, Darshan has worked with 75+ colleges and corporates across India — running the room himself rather than handing it off, from engineering campuses in Mandya to boardrooms with Landmark Worldwide's international teams.

His focus sits at the intersection of self-awareness and readiness: helping people understand who they are before asking them to perform as professionals, leaders, or job candidates.

How an Engagement Runs

A coaching journey, not a one-off session

Every program — whether a single workshop or a multi-week rollout — follows the same underlying path, adapted to the audience and time available.

01

Connect

Understand the group's real context — who they are, what they're walking into, and what's actually getting in their way.

02

Assess

Identify the gap between where the group stands today and the mindset or skill the outcome requires.

03

Design

Build a session or program sequence suited to the audience — students, freshers, faculty, or working professionals.

04

Deliver

Facilitate high-engagement, experiential sessions — people discover the shift themselves rather than being told about it.

05

Sustain

Reinforce what changed through follow-through and continued coaching, so the shift outlasts the session.
